Social video scheduling

Plan once. Publish short-form video everywhere, on time.

AI Lab Scheduler is a social media scheduling and publishing tool from AI Lab. Connect your TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts, put your videos on one calendar, and they go out at the times you choose.

TikTokYouTubeFacebookInstagram

What it does

Everything a small content team needs to keep a daily posting schedule without logging into four apps.

1

Connect your accounts

Sign in to each platform with its official login. You choose which accounts to connect and can disconnect them at any time.

2

Schedule from one calendar

Upload a video, write the caption and hashtags, pick the platforms and a time. Queue a week of posts in one sitting.

3

Publish automatically

At the scheduled time, posts are sent through each platform's official API, with no copy-pasting between apps.

4

Track how posts perform

See basic results such as views and likes next to each post, so you know what to make more of.

Who it's for

Built for creators and small teams that publish short-form video every day.

  • Creators running video series who post a new episode on a fixed daily schedule.
  • Small brands and shops that promote products with short videos across several networks.
  • Social media managers handling a handful of accounts for one business.
  • Anyone tired of posting by hand to four apps at the same time every night.

Your accounts, your content

You keep ownership of everything you upload. AI Lab Scheduler only uses your connected accounts to do what you ask: publish the posts you scheduled and show you how they performed. You can revoke access at any time from the app or from each platform's settings. Full details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
